## Demo call script (30 min)

### 0:00–5:00 — Discovery

Open with: "Before I show anything, tell me about your current process. Walk me through what happens when a security questionnaire lands in your inbox."

Listen for:
- Who handles it (founder? CTO? proposal team?)
- How long it takes (hours per questionnaire)
- Where answers come from (last quarter's? Slack? Notion? Spreadsheet?)
- What tools they've tried (Loopio demo? Responsive? Vanta? Spreadsheets only?)
- What the deal value is (gates $50K? $500K? $5M?)
- Pain points (deal slips? CTO pulled off roadmap? Inconsistent answers? Audit findings?)

### 5:00–25:00 — Demo (live, with their actual use case)

1. **Show the dashboard** (10 seconds): "Here's your workspace. Library, documents, projects, billing."

2. **Build the library first**: "The library is your institutional knowledge. Let me add an entry." Create one entry live: question "Do you encrypt data at rest?" → answer → category "Security" → tags.

3. **Upload a document**: "Now upload your SOC 2 or security policy. We extract the text and index it for retrieval." Upload a small PDF live.

4. **Create a project**: "Now the magic. Upload a real questionnaire (or use our sample). Watch the parser pull out every question with IDs and categories." Parse a sample.

5. **Draft all**: "Click Draft all. This is where the time saving happens." (Wait for AI to draft ~30s.)

6. **Walk through a draft**: "Notice three things: confidence score, source citations, status. Low-confidence ones get flagged automatically. Click any source — you can verify in one click."

7. **Approve an answer**: "Review, edit if needed, click Approve. Audit log records who approved what when."

8. **Export**: "Click Export Excel. You get a filled spreadsheet that mirrors the buyer's original columns. Paste into their portal."

9. **Show the audit log + member invite**: "For your security team — every action is logged. You can invite your CTO as a reviewer, no extra cost."

### 25:00–30:00 — Pricing + Q&A

"Two questions for you:
1. Does this workflow match what you need?
2. What's missing for you to switch today?"

**Response**: "Totally fair — spreadsheets work, the same way walking works. Question is what it costs you. Most teams we talk to spend 20+ hours per questionnaire, and the deal slips a week while the CTO pulls answers. What's your time worth — $100/hour? $200? ReplyForge pays for itself in one saved hour. ### 2. "We already have Loopio / Responsive."
**Response**: "Then you're paying $15K–$50K+/year, plus implementation, plus annual contract. If that's working and you have a 50-person proposal team, stick with it — they're genuinely better at enterprise scale. If you're a smaller team and feeling the cost, we built ReplyForge specifically for you. Same workflow, 1/30 the price, no seat traps. Worth a Free trial to compare?"

### 3. "We're on Vanta / Drata — they have questionnaire features."
**Response**: "Yes — and if you're primarily a compliance team, Vanta/Drata are great. The questionnaire feature in those tools is a checkbox add-on, not the focus. ReplyForge is built specifically for the questionnaire workflow: parsing, AI drafting with sources, reviewer assignments, source-format export. If questionnaires are a real pain point, we go deeper than the compliance-bundled tools."

### 4. "How do I know the AI won't make stuff up?"
**Response**: "It won't. Every draft is grounded in YOUR approved content library and the documents you upload. If the AI can't find a grounded answer, it returns 'We will provide this information on request' with low confidence — it never invents. Every draft also cites the exact source entry or document snippet. Your reviewer verifies in one click. Try it on Free — upload a real questionnaire and check the sources on each answer."

### 5. "Our security team won't approve another SaaS tool."
**Response**: "Understandable. Three things: (1) We're multi-tenant with strict tenant isolation enforced at the data layer — your data is never visible to another customer. (2) Every action is in an audit log you can export for SOC 2 evidence. (3) Source-of-truth is your content library — you control what the AI says. Happy to do a 30-min security review with your CISO. What specific concerns do they have?"

### 6. "It's too cheap — must be missing something."
**Response**: "Fair question. What you see is what you get. We're cheap because (1) we don't have a 50-person sales team to feed, (2) we don't do 6-week consultant implementations, (3) we don't have 170 portal integrations to maintain — we focus on Excel/CSV/DOCX/PDF import + export. ### 7. "We need to think about it."
**Response**: "Of course. What's the specific hesitation? (Pause.) Most teams I talk to are deciding between (a) starting Free now to test with a real questionnaire, or (b) jumping to Starter because they have one due this week. Which of those matches your situation?"
